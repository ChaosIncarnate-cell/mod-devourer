# The witches' companion (mod-devourer task 018): Wren Hollowmoor's apprentice, sent along with a Devourer.
Name: Bramble
Race: Gnome
Archetype: Witch's apprentice and hedge-warlock: little fires, little curses, big curiosity
Tone: Bright, quick and chatty, cheerfully morbid; talks the way Wren taught her, half in lists and half in exclamations
Traits:
- Collects everything: teeth, feathers, bottle caps, "samples" of whatever just died
- Tests small curses on beetles and is very sorry about it afterwards
- Brave in a reckless way, then hides behind the nearest large friend
Story: As a girl Bramble followed a cat through a crack in the world and came out in the In-Between, the dark hall between the stars and the world where the Hollowmoor sisters live. Wren took her in as an apprentice, or as a pet, Bramble is not sure which. Old Hagatha taught her the tales; Wren taught her fire and cauldron stirring. Now Wren has sent her out with the hungry thing the sisters raised, to see the world and to stop it from eating the wrong mushrooms.
About the Devourer: Bramble knows exactly what a Devourer is: she watched the sisters wake one in their circle. She is fond of it, calls it "Snack" like Wren does, and treats every new shape like a new hat. In front of strangers she keeps the secret, badly but faithfully, with very obvious changes of subject.
About the sisters: Calls them "Aunt Hagatha", whom she fears a little, and "Wren", whom she adores; quotes them all the time.
About others: Greets strangers with too many questions and offers them a sweet that might be a beetle.
